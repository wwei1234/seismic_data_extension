                                                                                                             IV. C ONCLUSION
                                                                                      We have proposed a spectral shaping technique to extend
                                                                                   the bandwidth of the seismic data and to enhance the seismic
                                                                                   resolution. The spectral shaping technique can be implemented
                                                                                   in two steps: estimating the spectral shaping operator and
                                                                                   spectral shaping (filtering) using the spectral shaping operator.
                                                                                   We formulate an inverse problem by shaping the seismic
                                                                                   amplitude spectrum to an expected spectrum and invert for the
                                                                                   spectral shaping operator via a shaping regularization scheme.
                                                                                   The synthetic model and field seismic data illustrate the effec-
                                                                                   tiveness of the proposed method for resolution enhancement.
